# Process overview

I wanted a course that students would remember immediately, built around the fantasy of becoming a mastermind. The attraction was learning unusual skills together: observation, memory, mechanisms, electronics, digital investigation and coordinating a crew. My view of a good course is that its theme should give students a reason to practise, while each week changes what they can do in the same larger problem. I also wanted good reasoning to matter more than a lucky outcome.

My first approved plan emphasised decisions under uncertainty. The harness in [4c8495d](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Adithya-Rama/commit/4c8495d) and dossier implementation in [bc31705](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Adithya-Rama/commit/bc31705) made that coherent across twelve weeks. However, I was dissatisfied: the course had drifted from acquiring distinctive skills toward describing and organising a fictional plan. More atmosphere would not resolve that mismatch. I asked to reconsider the curriculum before rebuilding the website.

The breakthrough was making the learning experience the unit of design. I asked for interactive three-dimensional labs and assessments, with practice, a skill check and application under changed conditions. The academy in [8aca1cd](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Adithya-Rama/commit/8aca1cd) turns that direction into twelve capability models and Operation Last Light. A mechanism has conditions that affect its movement; an electrical diagnosis depends on recorded measurements; a changed source can invalidate an earlier choice. The course still teaches judgement, but through something the student can attempt, inspect and revise.

I encoded that position in [CLAUDE.md](CLAUDE.md): every week needs a distinct skill, explanatory feedback, an unfamiliar transfer task and an output used later. The course-specific checks in [spec/curriculum.test.ts](spec/curriculum.test.ts) protect those connections, published dates and assessment requirements. The [training tests](spec/training-engine.test.ts) protect causal rules and changed configurations. I retained conventional navigation and complete static materials because entering a game should not be a prerequisite for understanding an assignment. The agent could choose implementation details within the fixed Astro platform; it could not replace the curriculum with disconnected activities.

When I said the academy direction worked, I also asked whether students would understand the skill, genuinely practise it and want to return. My next instruction was specific: provide a complete, similar but different example for every lab and assessment, like a demonstration website accompanying a website assignment. In [454d568](https://github.com/comp4020-agentic-coding-studio/comp4020-ass2-Adithya-Rama/commit/454d568), that becomes sixteen demonstrations with Watch and Take Control modes and finished submissions. The harness requires the depicted apparatus to match the explanation, while [demonstration tests](spec/demonstration.test.ts) keep observation separate from successful practice and assessed progress.

Verification had to test those promises. The agent's [rendered review](docs/evidence/demo-final-visual-notes.md) found a connector hidden by the apparatus and furniture covering route cells; both were corrected. The final [validation record](docs/DEMONSTRATION-VALIDATION.md) documents completed learner-control journeys on desktop and phone, preserved coursework, and readable captions. These are agent observations and automated evidence, not student playtesting I claim to have conducted. They support functional acceptance; they do not establish that the teaching is enjoyable.

I deliberately left originality, appeal, prose quality and the value of the skills to human judgement. My role is to decide what is worth learning and reject work that misses that purpose, even when it is substantial and passes checks. The outcome I want remains the same: students arrive for the mastermind fantasy and leave better able to understand systems, test assumptions and revise decisions. Publication must still be verified separately from local success.
