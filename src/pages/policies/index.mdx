## What this course is

MASTERMIND is an original fictional course at Slop University. Its bank, characters and systems are invented. Exercises use symbolic rules and supplied documents. They require no real security testing, physical lock work or contact with an outside target.

The website is an open prototype of the student experience. It contains no enrolment, live staff messaging, online grading or assignment submission service.

## Collaboration and contribution

Weekly Fieldwork and A1 are individual. You may discuss rules, but your artefacts and explanation must be your own. A2 is completed in pairs. The final project normally combines two pairs into a group of four.

Record responsibilities and contributions as work develops. If you disagree, state the contested decision, relevant evidence and agreed next step. If a member becomes unavailable, preserve the work already done and raise the situation during the fictional course's lab. An individual contribution remains assessable; absence does not erase it.

The capstone's group criteria supply 80% of the task mark. The individual defence supplies 20% separately for each member. There is no automatic multiplier based on peer popularity.

## Assistance, AI and attribution

Declare substantial assistance, including AI-generated material. Cite the source and explain how you checked it. A fluent answer is not evidence that a rule was applied correctly.

Copying a supplied exemplar into your dossier does not make it your original reasoning. Samples are labelled teaching material. You may analyse or adapt them with attribution, but the assessment asks what you understand and can defend.

## Feedback

Activities provide immediate rule-based feedback and exemplars. They do not assign academic marks to open-ended explanations. The lab includes peer and tutor discussion.

Major-task feedback is scheduled within seven days of the deadline. A2 feedback should inform the final group operation. If feedback does not make sense, bring the exact sentence and the part of your work it concerns.

## Deadlines and extensions

All due times use **Australia/Canberra**. Weekly records are due on the Friday date shown on each lab at 17:00. Best-ten marking accommodates two missed records; the fieldwork portfolio closes on 28 May 2027 at 17:00.

One declared 48-hour extension per student is available across the major assignments, capped at the teaching-period end. For a group task, agree the extension with the group and record it together. It does not silently change the other team's deadline. Later or final-week arrangements require an individual arrangement with the fictional teaching team.

The teaching period ends on 28 May. A standard extension cannot move the final project beyond it. Raise final-week needs before the deadline. These are the designed SlopU course rules, not ANU's COMP4020 submission policies.

## Access and alternatives

All required content is readable without completing earlier games. Later weeks supply a labelled starting example. Spoilers are visible because revision, not secrecy, is the learning objective.

Use keyboard buttons to reorder symbols. Diagrams have text equivalents. Timing is optional; no reaction speed is marked. Every lab includes a paper or document worksheet and a worked explanation. A written reconstruction can replace the interactive rehearsal when needed, using the same six decisions and rules.

If a task is inaccessible, preserve the learning outcome while changing the medium. Reading speed, pointer precision and animation tolerance are not criteria.

## Saving and privacy

Practice is stored in the current browser after you press Save. There are no accounts or shared crews. A different browser or device does not automatically have your dossier.

Export a JSON backup to move or restore work. Markdown exports and print views provide readable records. Clearing browser data can remove saved work. If saving is blocked or full, the app keeps the current session in memory and offers export.

Imports are validated before replacing anything. Unsupported versions, unknown activities, oversized files and malformed JSON are rejected. Reset and replacement require confirmation. A corrupted existing browser record is protected until you explicitly replace it.

No activity sends your answers to an AI service or a grading server. Exporting a file does not submit an assignment.

## Getting help

Start with the worked example and source file, then the relevant assessment brief. In the fictional taught course, bring unresolved questions to Monday briefings or Friday labs. The [teaching profiles](/people/) explain their responsibilities; they do not imply live contact accounts.
