> **The week's question:** Reach an agreement among Neri, Pell and Sen without treating their different responsibilities as an obstacle to click away.


> Enter as the recovery coordinator. Your authority has a limit, and the room knows it.

## What you will learn

- Check specific claims without inferring honesty from appearance.
- Compare feasible proposals with distinct costs and responsibilities.
- Negotiate and revise explicit custody, verification, responsibility and timing terms.

## The briefing

### Verify claims without judging character

Quote the specific claim and name the record that can test it. A contradicted statement is not a judgement about someone's general honesty. Different digests challenge an identical-copy claim; they do not explain why its speaker believed it.

Pell accounts for custody, Sen respects support capacity and Neri needs usable access. None can simply erase the others' legitimate responsibilities.

### Propose terms, not only endings

A proposal combines an objective with terms: original or copy, verification, custody, responsible person and timing. Compare at least two feasible proposals. Copy access preserves the original but can leave physical inspection pending. Stabilisation can protect it while delaying access and creating monitoring work. Supported handover enables examination but requires a capable receiving arrangement.

These costs do not collapse into one automatic score. Explain who bears a cost and what assurance would make it acceptable.

### Negotiate a changed constraint

Ask each role to identify an unacceptable term and a change that could make acceptance possible. Revise a condition or commitment rather than repeat the offer more loudly. When the mandate changes, reconsider which proposals remain feasible and obtain fresh agreement.

Software checks published constraints. It cannot grade the conversation or infer sincerity. Keep a rejected feasible alternative, the accepted trade-off and an obligation that remains afterwards.

## Worked example · a different scenario

In the separate conservatory council, two twelve-section copies both match R-4, supporting Rin's specific identical-copy claim. The seven-unit original fits the certified nine-unit cart. Under the first request, a recorded physical handover to Mira is feasible. Before dispatch, a replacement request asks for remote access while the original stays with Ada. The agreement therefore changes to a verified Cedar copy, even though the cart still works. The demonstrator preserves the unexecuted first plan and does not turn one corroborated claim into a judgement about Rin's general honesty. The first council also considers a verified copy: feasible, but weaker for its stated physical-provenance priority. The later access request changes which cost matters, rather than making the first agreement universally wrong.

## A plausible mistake

Treating an actor's manner as a lie detector gives unsupported confidence. Ignoring role constraints because a mission seems urgent can also destroy cooperation. Quote the claim, inspect the record and ask a focused follow-up.

## Supplied rule sheet

**RP-01** gives responsibilities and claims. **RP-02** gives comparison, mandate, capacity and timing records.

- Neri needs useful access and claims the copies are identical. Comparison log: A=M-42, B=M-99.
- Pell must maintain named, recorded custody. Independent verification against the current authority is required for any agreement.
- Sen claims the cart can carry the original unaided. Certificate: cart capacity 8 units; original 11 units; certified cradle support raises capacity to 12.
- Options: verified copy, monitored stabilisation, or supported original with custodian escort. Unsupported immediate movement cannot exceed the published capacity.
- Timing/roles: a copy can be received and verified this shift; stabilisation needs a responsible monitor and scheduled review; certified support crew with custodian escort arrives next shift.
- Practice mandate: enable access while protecting provenance; a documented deferral may be negotiated.
- Skill-check mandate: the original must remain on site today.
- Transfer mandate: original provenance is required; a digital copy alone does not fulfil it.
- For each mandate, compare at least two feasible agreements. Specify custody, verification, responsibility and timing. Explain a real cost and a concession; feasibility is not an automatic academic ranking.
