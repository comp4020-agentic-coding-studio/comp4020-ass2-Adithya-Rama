> Nadi does not leave the table. That makes the refusal harder to dismiss.

## What you will learn

- Interpret the scope of a refusal precisely.
- Revise responsibilities and consent as separate decisions.

## The briefing

Dissent is information about the plan’s social dependencies. E09 withdraws agreement to extraction pending an explanation of consequences. It does not prohibit discussion or grant automatic agreement to another objective. Record the refusal’s scope before proposing a response.

Reassigning a responsibility may solve a capacity problem, but it does not answer an objection to the objective. A new agreement must state what each person accepted and how it differs from the old one. A crew can decide to pause without dissolving; it can also revise the mission while preserving some roles.

## Worked example

Tess retains coordination while the council reads E10. Nadi agrees to discuss disclosure, and the group records that a decision is still pending. This is more precise than marking the whole crew ‘ready’ because everyone remains present.

## A plausible mistake

Reading presence as consent hides the very disagreement the course asks you to handle. Treating Nadi as a broken component similarly confuses a person’s judgement with a missing capability.

## Before the lab

Read E09 and revisit the Week 5 agreement (20 minutes). Identify which statements it can no longer support. The full [case-file library](/resources/#E09) is available without sign-in.

Assign one person to present the objective, one to state the refusal, and one to record only explicit agreements. Rotate before writing the final agreement.
