## Before you arrive

Read E09 and revisit the Week 5 agreement (20 minutes). Identify which statements it can no longer support.

Bring crew agreement. If you are entering this week directly, use the labelled example in the activity. It supplies a starting point and does not count as your original work.

## The two-hour lab

| Time | Work |
| --- | --- |
| 0–15 min | Read the case file and name the decision the evidence affects. |
| 15–35 min | Assign one person to present the objective, one to state the refusal, and one to record only explicit agreements. Rotate before writing the final agreement. |
| 35–70 min | Complete the playable exercise below; record a reason before inspecting the feedback. |
| 70–95 min | Exchange explanations. Challenge one assumption using a specific source. |
| 95–110 min | Revise the output and explain which part of the feedback changed it. |
| 110–120 min | Save or export the record and write the closing reflection. |

## What leaves the room

**revised-crew-agreement** is this week's named artefact. The revised crew agreement governs the Divided Crew preset and the final project’s contribution account.

Include your decision, the case-file identifier, a justification, and one remaining limitation. The fields in the exercise are a starting structure; expand them in your assessment document where the brief requires it.

## How to judge the response

Reading presence as consent hides the very disagreement the course asks you to handle. Treating Nadi as a broken component similarly confuses a person’s judgement with a missing capability.

A strong response explains a particular choice and an alternative. The supplied exemplar is inspectable feedback, not a sentence you must copy. Your confidence or reflection is never assigned an automatic academic grade.

## Closing reflection

What changed because you listened to the refusal rather than replacing the person?
