## Before you arrive

Read E11 and bring your current mission, model and crew agreement (20 minutes).

Bring plan v2, revised crew agreement, revised mission. If you are entering this week directly, use the labelled example in the activity. It supplies a starting point and does not count as your original work.

## The two-hour lab

| Time | Work |
| --- | --- |
| 0–15 min | Read the case file and name the decision the evidence affects. |
| 15–35 min | Run Baseline and one contrast. Exchange exports with another team; their challenge must name a source and a particular checkpoint. |
| 35–70 min | Complete the playable exercise below; record a reason before inspecting the feedback. |
| 70–95 min | Exchange explanations. Challenge one assumption using a specific source. |
| 95–110 min | Revise the output and explain which part of the feedback changed it. |
| 110–120 min | Save or export the record and write the closing reflection. |

## What leaves the room

**revision-memo** is this week's named artefact. Two contrasting records and a revision memo become part of the final project.

Include your decision, the case-file identifier, a justification, and one remaining limitation. The rehearsal export belongs beside your written reasoning; the ending does not earn the mark.

## How to judge the response

Calling a run successful because it reaches extraction confuses the outcome with the quality of the reasoning. Selecting every preset without comparing a decision is activity, not analysis.

A strong response explains a particular choice and an alternative. The supplied exemplar is inspectable feedback, not a sentence you must copy. Your confidence or reflection is never assigned an automatic academic grade.

## Closing reflection

Which criticism did your revision answer, and which remains open?
