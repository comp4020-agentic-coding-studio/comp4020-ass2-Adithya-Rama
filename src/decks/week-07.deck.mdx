## The unexpected results

A maintainer can read embargoed research.

A reader cannot read it.

Both results contradict this charter.

```notes
These are the alternate observatory's two faults. Record the role, requested action, expected result and observed result before changing a cell. One fault grants too much; the other blocks required work.
```

## State this charter first

Readers may read research.

Maintainers may service equipment.

Custodians may read and approve release.

```notes
The maintainer may not read embargoed research. Every unlisted combination is denied. Have students explain why equipment responsibility does not imply permission to see its research data.
```

## Identity and authority

Identity answers **who**.

Permission answers **may they do this**.

A job title does not grant every right.

```notes
Explain why this model recognises the maintainer but still denies research reading. All examples remain in the local fictional simulator.
```

## Preserve both failures

Maintainer → read: **deny expected, allow observed**.

Reader → read: **allow expected, deny observed**.

```notes
A useful audit retains both excess access and missing access. Ask what would be lost if the original policy were overwritten before these results were recorded.
```

<DeckNav slide={7} total={14} />

## Make the two repairs

Deny maintainer research reading.

Restore reader research reading.

Preserve the seven valid decisions.

```notes
Do not remove maintainer service or custodian duties. Students should connect each edit to one failed expected-versus-observed test, rather than changing a whole role at once.
```

## Denying everything fails too

No unauthorised operation succeeds.

But the reader cannot do required work.

Useful access is part of the contract.

```notes
Ask what a test suite containing only forbidden cases would miss. Connect the reader's false denial to the need for positive as well as negative tests.
```

## Recheck all nine cases

Reader reading succeeds.

Maintainer reading fails; service succeeds.

Custodian reading and approval succeed.

```notes
Check the other denied combinations as well. Retain the before/after tables with the same governing charter so the repair is reproducible.
```

## A release hold arrives

Custodian approval is suspended.

Research reading is unchanged.

Maintainer service is unchanged.

```notes
This signed notice changes an authorised expectation. It is not evidence the earlier repair was wrong. Preserve Version 1 under the old charter, then record Version 2 under the release hold.
```
