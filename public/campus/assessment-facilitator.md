# Assessment facilitator — requested model results

This sheet supports the assigned A1, A2 and Operation Last Light paper routes. It is not a worked-example submission or a sequence students should copy. Give the student case file first; reveal a result when the learner names a test and predicts what it would distinguish. Solo learners may reveal a requested result themselves and disclose that support.

## Circuit measurements (A2 and final)

The authored local supply is 6 V. The initial model contains an interrupted fuse and an open load switch. Do not announce the fault before the learner requests evidence.

| Requested test | Authored result |
| --- | --- |
| Supply measurement | 6 V at the source |
| Fuse continuity while isolated | No complete path through the original fuse |
| Load with original fuse | Off, regardless of switch position |
| Load with intact replacement and open switch | Off |
| Load with intact replacement and closed switch | On |
| Measurement after replacement | Intact path; it does not reconstruct missing pre-repair evidence |

Reject a replacement while the switch is closed. Retain the original readings. Ask what a dark load alone can and cannot identify. A successful repair needs a verification after power restoration.

Equipment Failure removes the transport lift supply; it does not remove the separate local transmission/support supply.

## Mechanism observations (A1 and final)

Apply output = 12 ÷ follower teeth × input turns; output direction is opposite. An engaged holding brake prevents rotation. A matching ratio with no actual input turn is only a prediction.

A1 requires half-speed output. Final support requires the output named by the verified profile. Reveal the observed output of the selected configuration; ask students to compare it with the source, rather than naming a gear to fit.

## Receipt and stable handover

Issue RECEIPT-A36 after verified profile A transmission, or RECEIPT-B48 after profile B transmission. Record collection, receiving-terminal check and handover. Do not issue a receipt for a selected but unverified source.

Stabilisation requires the matched support, working local supply, inspected stable indicator, named custodian and read-back. The written record must name monitoring responsibility, an escalation condition and remaining work. A facilitator cannot certify its reasoning merely because every form field is filled.

## Feedback boundaries

Show which governing condition a test meets or misses. Do not provide the next repair automatically. Offer a labelled hint if requested, keep the attempt record and assess explanation using the published rubric. No timing or physical precision is required.
